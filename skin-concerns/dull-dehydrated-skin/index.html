Skin concern guide

Dull, dehydrated or stressed skin

Skin that looks flat or feels tight usually needs hydration and gentle exfoliation. These appointments focus on both.

HydraFacials are the quickest route: the Express for a 45-minute refresh, the Platinum for the full experience. The Glass Skin Facial is customized around hydration and glow.

If dehydration keeps coming back, the Hydrate Mesotherapy microneedling option is designed for it. Katrina also works on damaged skin barriers, so mention any stinging or sensitivity at your consultation.
