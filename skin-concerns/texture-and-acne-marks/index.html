Skin concern guide

Texture, acne marks and scarring

Rough texture, post-acne marks and the look of acne scarring respond to different treatments. This guide lays out the options on the ABK SKIN menu.

Microneedling is the menu's main option for the appearance of acne scarring and enlarged pores. It works gradually and is planned after a skin assessment. Enhanced microneedling adds a targeted serum.

For post-acne marks and surface roughness, BioRePeel and The Perfect Derma Peel are both options. Dermaplaning gives an instant smoothness for events. On the body, the Keratosis Pilaris Treatment targets rough, bumpy arms.
