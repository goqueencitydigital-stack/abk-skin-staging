Skin concern guide

Puffiness and facial tension

Puffiness after travel, a salty dinner or a long week is one of the easiest concerns to address in a single visit.

For the face, choose the Lymphatic Drainage Facial for gentle drainage, or The Sculpting Facial if you also clench or carry tension in your jaw. The Platinum HydraFacial starts with lymphatic drainage too.

If the puffiness is in your arms, legs, abdomen or back, Full Body Lymphatic Drainage covers those areas.

When to see a doctor: Sudden or one-sided swelling, or swelling with pain, should be checked by a doctor.
