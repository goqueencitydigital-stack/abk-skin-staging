Skin concern guide

Uneven skin tone and dullness

Dullness, dark spots and uneven-looking tone are among the most common reasons clients book. The options differ mostly in downtime.

If you want brightening with little visible peeling, the BioRePeel Brightening Facial is designed for that and is usually done as a series. The Perfect Derma Peel goes further, with peeling that can last up to a week.

Customized facials such as the Glass Skin Facial or a Platinum HydraFacial with a brightening booster are gentler options for regular maintenance. Daily sunscreen matters for all of them.

When to see a doctor: A new or changing dark spot should be checked by a dermatologist.
