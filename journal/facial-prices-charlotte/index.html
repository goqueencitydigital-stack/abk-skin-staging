If you have searched "how much is a facial in Charlotte," you have probably noticed that prices range widely and that many spa menus make it hard to compare. In short

  • Facials in Charlotte at ABK SKIN start at $125 for The Basic B Facial, a 45-minute refresh.
  • Most customized 60-minute facials are $175, including the Glass Skin Facial, Acne Facial, Dermaplaning Facial, Lymphatic Drainage Facial and Sculpting Facial.

  • Corrective treatments include BioRePeel and The Perfect Derma Peel at $295, Microneedling at $395 and Enhanced Microneedling at $550.
  • Memberships bring the monthly cost down: ABK GLOW CLUB is $149 a month and ABK SKIN CLUB is $199 a month.

Consultation prices

Not sure where to start? ABK SKIN offers two kinds of consultation.

  • Skin Consultation (60 minutes, in studio). The booking menu does not list a price, so check it before you confirm.
  • Virtual consultation ($100). The fee is applied toward purchases, so it works as a starting credit.

A consultation is a smart first step if you have several concerns, are thinking about a series of peels or microneedling, or want a home routine built around the products you already own.

What affects the cost of a facial in Charlotte

Facial prices across Charlotte vary for a few reasons, and knowing them helps you compare fairly.

Time with your aesthetician

A 45-minute facial and a 90-minute facial are different services. Longer appointments allow more steps, more massage and more targeted work.

Products and technology

Medical-grade products, HydraFacial serums and boosters, LED light therapy and professional peel solutions cost more than basic spa products. At ABK SKIN, treatments use professional lines such as Revision Skincare and Hydrinity.

Customization and expertise

Each ABK SKIN facial starts with a look at your skin that day. A licensed aesthetician choosing the right exfoliation, extractions and finishing steps is part of what you pay for. McKensi West, Master Medical Aesthetician, brings 12 years of experience, and you can book with McKensi directly.

Results that build over time

Some treatments, such as BioRePeel and microneedling, are designed as a series. The per-visit price matters, but so does the plan. Ask your aesthetician how many sessions make sense for your goals.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a facial in Charlotte?

At ABK SKIN, facials start at $125 for The Basic B Facial. Most 60-minute customized facials are $175, the Express HydraFacial is $195 and the 90-minute ABK Glow Facial is $325.

How much is a HydraFacial in Charlotte?

The Express HydraFacial is $195 and the ABK Hydra-Peel, which layers a chemical peel over a Platinum HydraFacial, is $395. The Platinum HydraFacial shows "View current pricing" on this site, and the booking page shows the current amount.

How much does microneedling cost in Charlotte?

Microneedling at ABK SKIN is $395. Enhanced Microneedling, which adds a professional serum such as PDRN, is $550.

How much is a chemical peel in Charlotte?

The BioRePeel Brightening Facial and The Perfect Derma Peel are each $295 at ABK SKIN. They differ in downtime, so read the comparison guide before you book.

Is a facial membership worth it?

If you book a facial most months, usually yes. ABK GLOW CLUB is $149 a month and ABK SKIN CLUB is $199 a month, each with a 3-month minimum and no setup fee, plus a product discount.

Do I need to tip?

Tipping policies are not listed on the ABK SKIN booking menu. If you are unsure, ask the studio when you book.

Why is there no price listed for some services?

A few services, such as the Back Facial and the in-studio Skin Consultation, do not show a price on the booking menu. That does not mean they are free. The booking page shows the current price before you confirm.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellations made less than 24 hours before an appointment are subject to a $50 charge.
