A few products in the ABK SKIN shop look alike but are different items, each with its own price and purchase page. Ordering the wrong strength of a retinol or the wrong finish of a tinted sunscreen is frustrating, especially because all product sales are final. This guide explains how to tell them apart and how to choose.

When in doubt, ask Katrina at your next appointment or by email at abkskinn@gmail.com before you order. A quick question saves you from guessing.

In short

  • Retinol Complete comes in 0.5 ($118) and 1.0 ($138). Each strength has its own purchase page.
  • Intellishade comes in three versions at $89 each: Original and Matte, both SPF 45, and the sheer, all-mineral TruPhysical Clear, SPF 50.
  • YouthFull Lip Replenisher comes clear ($44.50) or tinted ($46.50).
  • Hydrinity's Renewing HA Serum ($140) and Restorative HA Serum ($120) are different products with different goals.
  • Travel sizes of Prelude and both HA serums are their own products with their own prices.
  • Kits bundle two steps. Check the contents if you already own one of the items.

Why the details matter

Skincare is personal. The same ingredient can feel wonderful at one strength and irritating at another, and a finish that looks perfect on one person can look shiny or flat on someone else. Choosing well also protects your budget. Because all product sales at ABK SKIN are final, it is worth spending a minute on the details before you check out.

Retinol Complete: 0.5 or 1.0

Revision Skincare's Retinol Complete is a retinol serum designed to brighten and smooth the look of skin's texture. According to Revision Skincare, it uses time-released retinol for minimal irritation, alongside hydrating ingredients to help combat the dryness associated with retinol, plus bakuchiol and THD ascorbate, a form of vitamin C.

It is sold in two strengths, both 1 fl oz:

StrengthPriceTypically chosen for
Retinol Complete 0.5$118Newer retinol users or more sensitive skin
Retinol Complete 1.0$138Skin that already tolerates retinol well

Revision describes the two strengths as "based on skin's tolerability." On this site, both strengths share one page, and each has its own button that opens its own purchase page in the ABK shop. The strength number is printed on the label, so check it when your order arrives.

How to choose

  • New to retinol? Start with 0.5. You can always move up.
  • Already using a retinol without irritation? Ask whether 1.0 makes sense for you.
  • Sensitive, pregnant or breastfeeding? Ask before using any retinoid. Retinoids are generally avoided during pregnancy, so check with your doctor.

Other retinoid options

If you want a gentler step in, Revision Skincare's D·E·J Night Face Cream ($190) pairs time-released 0.25% retinol with bakuchiol in a hydrating cream. Hydrinity's RetaXome Daily Retinal Hydrator ($145) and RetaXome Retinal Boost Treatment Pads ($90) use retinal instead of retinol. Our fine lines guide explains where retinoids fit in a routine.

Intellishade: Original, Matte or TruPhysical Clear

Intellishade is Revision Skincare's tinted moisturizer with sunscreen, and the ABK shop carries three versions, each $89 for 1.7 oz. Each has its own page, linked to the others so you can compare.

VersionSun protectionFinishGood for
Intellishade OriginalBroad-spectrum SPF 45Tinted, natural glowMost skin types who want a sheer tint
Intellishade MatteBroad-spectrum SPF 45Tinted, matteOily or combination skin, or anyone who dislikes shine
Intellishade TruPhysical ClearBroad-spectrum SPF 50, 100% mineralSheerClients who prefer mineral sunscreen without a white cast

Revision describes Original as a 5-in-1 tinted moisturizer formulated to correct, protect, conceal, brighten and hydrate, and Matte as inspired by the Original formula with a matte finish. TruPhysical Clear is described as a sheer, all-mineral formula that blends without a white cast or shine. Read the details on each page for the sunscreen filters each one uses.

How to choose

  • Love a glow? Original.
  • Shiny by lunchtime? Matte.
  • Prefer mineral sunscreen? TruPhysical Clear.

Whichever you choose, daily sunscreen is one of the most important steps in any routine, especially if you use retinoids, acids or vitamin C. Our vitamin C guide explains how sunscreen and antioxidants work together.

YouthFull Lip Replenisher: clear or tinted

The YouthFull Lip Replenisher ($44.50, 1-pack) and the Tinted YouthFull Lip Replenisher ($46.50) are separate products. Revision describes the original as a lip plumper with hyaluronic acid that targets visible signs of lip aging. The tinted version hydrates, smooths and visibly volumizes while revealing your own pink tint through pH-reactive technology. Their pages link to each other.

  • Want a clear, everyday treatment? Choose the original.
  • Want a hint of color? Choose the tinted version.

Hydrinity HA serums: Renewing or Restorative

Hydrinity's Renewing HA Serum and Restorative HA Serum both use Hydrinity's PPM⁶ technology and hyaluronic acid, but they are different products with different goals.

SerumFull sizeTravel sizeHydrinity describes it as
Renewing HA Serum$140$80A super hydrating serum to reduce the signs of skin aging, boost radiance and improve elasticity
Restorative HA Serum$120$72Enhancing hydration, brightness and luminosity while helping delicate, compromised skin recover

How to choose

  • Focused on fine lines, radiance and firmness? Renewing is designed for that.
  • Recovering from a professional treatment, or skin that feels sensitized? Restorative is designed for delicate, compromised skin. It is the serum in the Restorative Post Procedure Kit.

Our peptides guide explains how peptides and hyaluronic acid work in products like these.

Travel sizes

Some favorites come in a travel size, each with its own page and price:

  • Prelude Facial Treatment Cleanser: full size $58, travel size $36.
  • Renewing HA Serum: full size $140, travel size $80.
  • Restorative HA Serum: full size $120, travel size $72.

Travel sizes are a good way to try a product before committing to the full size, or to keep your routine consistent on trips. Check stock on each product page before you plan around one.

Kits versus single products

Hydrinity's two kits each bundle two steps: Hyacyn Active, a hypochlorous acid treatment spray, plus an HA serum.

KitPriceWhat is in it
Age Renewal Kit$180Hyacyn Active and Renewing HA Serum
Restorative Post Procedure Kit$160Hyacyn Active and Restorative HA Serum

If you already own Hyacyn Active ($60) or one of the serums, check the kit contents before ordering. You may only need the single product. Hydrinity's instructions for both kits are the same: after cleansing, spray two to four times on the face and neck, let it dry, then apply the serum.

Checking price and stock

Prices and stock on this site are copied from the ABK shop on the date shown on each product page. The shop's own product page is always the final word on price, stock, tax and shipping. If an item shows as out of stock, contact ABK SKIN to ask when it will be back.

The takeaway

Most mix-ups come down to strength, finish or size. Check the label details on each product page, start gentle with retinoids, pick the Intellishade finish that suits your skin and choose the HA serum by goal. Because all product sales are final, ask ABK SKIN before you order if you are unsure. Browse the full ABK SKIN shop when you are ready.

Frequently asked questions

Which Retinol Complete strength should I start with?

Most people new to retinol start with 0.5 and move to 1.0 once their skin tolerates it. Ask your aesthetician if you are unsure.

What is the difference between Intellishade Original and Matte?

Both are tinted moisturizers with broad-spectrum SPF 45. Original has a natural glow, and Matte is inspired by the same formula with a matte finish.

Is Intellishade TruPhysical Clear tinted?

Revision describes it as a sheer, 100% mineral, broad-spectrum SPF 50 moisturizer that blends without a white cast. Read the product page for full details.

Can I return a product if I chose the wrong one?

All product sales are final, so ask ABK SKIN before you order if you are unsure about a strength, finish or size.

Is the travel size the same formula?

The Prelude and HA serum travel sizes carry the same product descriptions as the full sizes. Check each product page for details.

Do members get a discount on products?

Yes. ABK GLOW CLUB members get 10% off ABK SKIN products, and ABK SKIN CLUB members get 20%.
