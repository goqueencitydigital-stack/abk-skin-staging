Hyperpigmentation and dark spots: treatments in Charlotte and a routine that works

Dark spots and uneven skin tone are among the most common reasons people book a facial in Charlotte. Whether it is sun spots from years of summers outdoors, marks left behind after breakouts or general unevenness, hyperpigmentation can make skin look older or tired even when everything else is healthy.

The good news is that the appearance of hyperpigmentation can often be improved with the right combination of in-studio treatments, targeted skincare and consistent sun protection. This guide explains the common types of dark spots, the treatments ABK SKIN offers, a brightening routine built from products in the ABK SKIN shop and a realistic timeline.

In short

  • Hyperpigmentation is any area where skin looks darker than the skin around it, from sun spots to post-acne marks.
  • Daily sunscreen is the foundation. Without it, even the best treatments struggle to keep up.
  • In studio: a BioRePeel series ($295) for minimal downtime, The Perfect Derma Peel ($295) for stronger correction, or the ABK Hydra-Peel ($395).
  • At home: Vivid Brightening Serum, C+ Correcting Complex 30% and Intellishade SPF 45.
  • Be patient. Fading the look of pigment takes consistent care over months, not days.

What causes dark spots and hyperpigmentation?

Hyperpigmentation happens when skin produces extra melanin, the pigment that gives skin its color, in certain areas. Common types include:

TypeWhat it looks likeCommon trigger
Sun spotsFlat brown spots on the face, chest and handsYears of sun exposure
Post-inflammatory hyperpigmentationFlat marks left after breakouts, bites or irritationInflammation, such as acne
MelasmaLarger patches, often on the cheeks, forehead or upper lipHormones, heat and sun; diagnosis by a medical provider
General unevennessA blotchy or dull overall toneSun, dehydration and slow cell turnover

If a spot is changing in size, shape or color, is raised, bleeds or itches, see a dermatologist before treating it cosmetically. Melasma in particular can be stubborn and sensitive to heat and irritation, so it is worth getting a medical diagnosis.

Step one: sun protection

Every dark spot plan starts with daily sunscreen. UV exposure is one of the main triggers for pigment, and visible light can also play a role. Without consistent protection, new spots appear while you are fading old ones.

In-studio treatments for hyperpigmentation in Charlotte

BioRePeel Brightening Facial

BioRePeel exfoliates and renews the surface without the prolonged peeling of a traditional peel. It can help improve the appearance of dullness, uneven tone, rough texture and post-acne marks. ABK Hydra-Peel

The ABK Hydra-Peel layers a customized chemical peel over a Platinum HydraFacial to cleanse, exfoliate, refine, hydrate and brighten in one visit. It is $395 for about 90 minutes.

Microneedling

Microneedling is chosen to improve the appearance of uneven texture and tone, as well as acne scarring. The radiance option in Enhanced Microneedling adds a brightening-focused serum.

A brightening routine for dark spots

Morning

1. Cleanse gently. Prelude Facial Treatment Cleanser ($58) contains gentle resurfacing AHAs. 2. Vitamin C. C+ Correcting Complex 30% ($190) uses 30% THD ascorbate to visibly brighten and even skin tone. Read the vitamin C serum guide. 3. Brightening serum. Vivid Brightening Serum ($150) is a hydroquinone-free formula with hexylresorcinol, botanical AHAs, maqui fruit extract, peptides and hyaluronic acid. Ask your aesthetician whether to use it morning, evening or both. 4. Sunscreen. Intellishade, every day, reapplied when you are outdoors.

Evening

1. Cleanse. 2. Renewal step, a few nights a week. A retinoid such as Retinol Complete or RetaXome Daily Retinal Hydrator supports cell turnover. Start slowly; the retinol vs retinal guide explains how. 3. Hydrate. A hyaluronic acid serum and moisturizer keep your barrier comfortable while you use actives.

Do not start several strong products at once. Add one at a time, and read the over-exfoliation guide if your skin starts to sting or flake.

How long does it take to fade dark spots?

Fading the look of hyperpigmentation takes time because pigment sits within the skin and fades as cells turn over. Many people need consistent care for several months to see meaningful change, and deeper or hormonal pigment can take longer. A peel series or treatment plan can speed things up, but daily sunscreen is what keeps results from slipping.

Mistakes that make dark spots worse

  • Skipping sunscreen on cloudy days or in winter
  • Picking at breakouts, which can leave post-inflammatory marks
  • Over-exfoliating, which causes irritation, and irritation can trigger more pigment
  • Using too many actives at once
  • Expecting results in a week and switching products too often

The takeaway

Hyperpigmentation responds best to a plan: daily sun protection, a consistent brightening routine and in-studio treatments matched to your skin and schedule. Frequently asked questions

What is the best treatment for dark spots in Charlotte?

It depends on the type of pigment and how much downtime you can take. At ABK SKIN, a BioRePeel series suits clients who want minimal downtime, while The Perfect Derma Peel offers stronger correction with up to seven days of peeling.

How much is a dark spot treatment at ABK SKIN?

BioRePeel and The Perfect Derma Peel are each $295. The ABK Hydra-Peel is $395 and Microneedling is $395.

Can facials remove sun spots?

Professional peels and microneedling can improve the appearance of sun damage and uneven tone. Results depend on your skin, and daily sunscreen is essential to keep them.

Is Vivid Brightening Serum hydroquinone-free?

Yes. Hydrinity describes it as hydroquinone-free, with hexylresorcinol, botanical AHAs, peptides and patented hyaluronic acid.

Can I treat melasma with a chemical peel?

Melasma can be sensitive to heat and irritation. Get a diagnosis from a medical provider, and book a consultation so your aesthetician can recommend a gentle, appropriate plan.

How do I fade post-acne marks?

Prevent new breakouts, avoid picking, wear sunscreen daily and consider a BioRePeel series. Vitamin C and brightening serums can help at home.

Does vitamin C help with dark spots?

Vitamin C is used to visibly brighten and even the look of skin tone. C+ Correcting Complex 30% uses THD ascorbate for this purpose.

How long until I see results?

Expect several months of consistent care for meaningful change. Peels can speed things up, and sunscreen protects your progress.
