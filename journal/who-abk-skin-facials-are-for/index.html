Choosing an aesthetician is personal. You are trusting someone with your face, your budget and often your confidence. ABK SKIN asked the question directly on Instagram: "Am I the aesthetician for you?" This guide answers it in more detail, so you can decide before you book.

If you are searching for a facial in Charlotte, there are plenty of options, from large spas to medical practices. This guide explains what makes ABK SKIN different, who it suits best, who it may not suit, and how to choose your first appointment.

In short

  • ABK SKIN is a boutique, one-on-one studio inside Salon Lofts on Metropolitan Avenue in Charlotte, by appointment only.
  • Treatments are customized around your skin and goals, with education and a plan.
  • It suits people ready to treat their skin consistently, not just once a year.
  • It is not the place for a medical diagnosis or walk-in visits.
  • Not sure what to book? Start with a Skin Consultation, The Basic B Facial or the ABK Glow Facial.

A boutique, one-on-one studio

ABK SKIN is a boutique skin studio inside Salon Lofts on Metropolitan Avenue in Charlotte, near Trader Joe's. It is not a large spa with a rotating team. You see your aesthetician one-on-one in a private loft, appointments are by booking only, and treatments are built around you rather than pulled from a fixed menu script.

That setting suits some people perfectly, and it is worth knowing what it means in practice.

Who you will see

ABK SKIN is owned by Katrina, who pairs medical-grade facials and skincare with the help of her medical director. McKensi West, ABK SKIN's Master Medical Aesthetician, brings 12 years of experience in aesthetics. You can book with McKensi directly, or read more about ABK SKIN.

The ABK method

Every plan at ABK SKIN follows the same four steps:

  • Analyze. A close look at your skin, your routine and your goals.
  • Correct. Treatments chosen to address what you want to change.
  • Build. A home routine that supports the work done in the studio.
  • Maintain. Regular care to keep your results.

That is a process, not a one-time fix, and it shapes everything from the first conversation to the products you take home.

ABK SKIN is likely a great fit if you…

Want a treatment chosen for your skin, not a set routine

Most ABK SKIN facials are customized. The ABK Glow Facial has no fixed routine at all: your aesthetician builds each step around your goals and how your skin looks when you arrive. The Glass Skin Facial starts with a skin analysis, and The Acne Facial begins with a close look at what may be contributing to breakouts.

Like to understand what is happening and why

Expect education. Clients leave with home-care advice and, for ongoing concerns like breakouts, a plan to follow. If you like knowing why a step was chosen, you will feel at home.

Are ready to treat your skin consistently

Real change in skin usually comes from consistency: regular treatments and a home routine that supports them. The studio's memberships exist for exactly this reason. Our membership comparison explains how they work.

Want to feel more confident in your skin

On Instagram, ABK SKIN describes its main priority as helping clients face the world as confidently as they can. If that is your goal, rather than a quick pamper session once a year, you are in the right place.

Prefer a calm, private appointment

A private loft means no busy treatment floor. Many clients book The Sculpting Facial or a Lymphatic Drainage Facial as much for the reset as for the skin.

Want professional skincare to use at home

ABK SKIN carries professional skincare from brands including Hydrinity, Revision Skincare and The Perfect Derma. Your aesthetician can recommend products for your skin, and you can browse the ABK SKIN shop between visits.

ABK SKIN may not be the right fit if you…

  • Need a medical diagnosis or prescription. An aesthetician does not diagnose skin conditions. For a diagnosis or prescription treatment, see a doctor or dermatologist. ABK SKIN works with a medical director for its advanced services, and can work alongside your provider's advice.
  • Want walk-in availability. Visits are by appointment only.
  • Are looking for one treatment to fix everything overnight. Most concerns improve over a series of visits plus home care.

How to choose your first appointment

If you are not sure what you need

Book a Skin Consultation. It is sixty minutes in person to talk through your concerns, review the products you use now and leave with a personal plan. Bring your products or photos of the labels. Can't get to the studio? A virtual consultation by phone or video is $100, and the fee is applied toward any products or treatments you purchase. Our guide to what to ask at your first consultation helps you prepare.

If you want an easy introduction

The Basic B Facial ($135) is a 45-minute refresh with exfoliation of your choice, extractions, a custom mask and a facial massage. It is a good first facial and a simple way to get to know the studio.

If you love a deep clean

The Express HydraFacial ($195, 45 minutes) and Platinum HydraFacial (60 minutes) are comfortable, no-downtime facials with painless extractions. Our Platinum HydraFacial guide explains every step.

If you want the full experience

The ABK Glow Facial ($325) gives you ninety minutes of one-on-one time, with every step chosen at the appointment.

If you have a specific concern

Start from the concern rather than the treatment. ABK SKIN's skin concern guides cover breakouts and congestion, uneven tone and dark spots, texture and acne marks, fine lines and firmness, dull or dehydrated skin, and puffiness and tension. Each one points to the treatments chosen for that concern.

If you have an event coming up

Timing matters. Our guide to booking a facial before an event walks backwards from your date.

What to expect at your first visit

  • Find the studio. ABK SKIN is inside Salon Lofts at 1133 Metropolitan Avenue, Suite 150, Loft 2. The visit page has directions and parking details.
  • Arrive with your history. Past treatments, peels or prescriptions, and anything that has irritated your skin.
  • Mention upcoming events. Some treatments need lead time before a wedding or photos.
  • Ask questions. You should leave knowing what was done, why, and what to do at home.

The first visit guide covers booking, policies and what to expect step by step.

The bottom line

If you want a customized, educational, one-on-one approach and you are ready to treat your skin consistently, ABK SKIN is built for you. If you are still unsure, a consultation is the easiest way to find out, or take the Skin Care Quiz for a quick starting point.

Frequently asked questions

Where is ABK SKIN?

Inside Salon Lofts at 1133 Metropolitan Avenue, Suite 150, Loft 2, Charlotte, NC 28204, near Trader Joe's. Visits are by appointment.

Do I need a consultation before a facial?

No. You can book most facials directly. A consultation is a good choice if you are unsure what to book or want a full plan.

What is the best first facial at ABK SKIN?

Many first-time clients start with The Basic B Facial, a 45-minute refresh, or an Express HydraFacial. If you want every step customized, book the 90-minute ABK Glow Facial.

Is a first facial different from a regular one?

Your first visit includes more conversation about your skin, history and goals, so your aesthetician can tailor the treatment.

Can I bring my products?

Yes. Bring them or photos of the labels, especially to a consultation.

Does ABK SKIN take walk-ins?

No. Visits are by appointment only.

How do I book?

Use the Book buttons on this site. They open ABK SKIN's online booking, where you can see current availability and pricing.

Questions about your skin?

Email abkskinn@gmail.com or bring them to your next appointment.
