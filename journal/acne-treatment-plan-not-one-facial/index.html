Why acne needs a treatment plan, not just one facial

How ABK SKIN approaches breakouts with a plan that combines in-studio treatments, home care and patience, and which acne treatments to ask about.

ABK SKIN

Breakouts can affect how you feel about your whole face. It is tempting to book one facial and hope it clears everything. One facial can make a real difference, but lasting change usually comes from a plan.

ABK SKIN recently shared one client's progress on Instagram. The changes in the photo came from one Hydra-Peel specialized for acne plus at-home skincare recommendations, and they were only the first step in a full treatment plan built around her. As ABK SKIN put it, "We are just getting started."

This guide explains how that kind of plan works.

Why one facial is rarely the whole answer

Acne is influenced by many things: oil production, how skin sheds, bacteria, inflammation, hormones, stress, products and habits. A facial can clear congestion and calm the skin, but what happens at home over the following weeks matters just as much.

That is why The Acne Facial at ABK SKIN includes home-care education and a treatment plan to follow over time, not just the treatment itself.

Step one: understand what is going on

Every plan starts with a close look at your skin and your routine. Expect questions about:

  • What you use now. Bring your products or photos of the labels.
  • What you have tried. Past treatments, prescriptions and what irritated your skin.
  • Patterns. When breakouts appear and where.
  • Your goals and timeline, including any events.

Aestheticians do not diagnose medical conditions. If your acne is severe, painful, cystic or scarring, or it is not improving, see a dermatologist. ABK SKIN can work alongside your provider's treatment.

Step two: in-studio treatments

Depending on your skin, a plan might include:

The Acne Facial ($175)

Built around what your skin needs that day. That may include targeted exfoliation, extractions, calming or clarifying steps, hydration or LED. Some redness after extractions is common.

ABK Hydra-Peel ($395)

Katrina's signature facial: a Platinum HydraFacial layered with a customized chemical peel. It can be specialized for acne, which is how the client plan ABK SKIN shared on Instagram began. Downtime depends on the peel selected.

BioRePeel Brightening Facial ($295)

A peel-style treatment chosen for congestion and acne as well as post-acne marks and rough texture, with minimal visible peeling. ABK SKIN recommends a series of three to four sessions spaced 10 to 14 days apart.

Back Facial

Breakouts are not only on the face. The Back Facial includes a cleanse, lymphatic techniques, exfoliation and a targeted booster for congestion and uneven tone. No price is listed in the booking menu, so check current pricing when you book.

Step three: home care that supports the plan

A good acne routine is consistent and gentle, not harsh. Your aesthetician will tailor it, but it usually includes:

  • A cleanser suited to congested skin. Hydrinity's Prelude Facial Treatment Cleanser contains gentle AHAs and is designed for daily use.
  • Calming support. Hydrinity's Hyacyn Active is a hypochlorous acid spray designed to calm stressed skin.
  • Hydration. Acne-prone skin still needs moisture.
  • Daily sunscreen, especially while using exfoliating actives and to help prevent dark marks lingering.

What to avoid

  • Picking. It can increase the chance of marks.
  • Over-exfoliating. A damaged barrier can keep breakouts on repeat.
  • Changing everything at once. Introduce new products one at a time.

Step four: patience and follow-up

Skin takes time. A treatment plan typically spaces sessions out and checks in on how your skin responds, adjusting as you go. Some clients, like the one above, notice changes early; for others it takes longer. Results vary from person to person.

After breakouts: marks and texture

Once active breakouts are under control, many clients want to work on leftover marks and texture. That is where treatments like microneedling, BioRePeel and The Perfect Derma Peel can come in. See the texture and acne marks guide for more.

The takeaway

Acne responds best to a thoughtful plan: in-studio treatments chosen for your skin, a simple home routine you will actually follow, and regular check-ins. Start with The Acne Facial or a Skin Consultation.

Frequently asked questions

Can a facial cure acne?

No facial can promise that. Professional treatments and a consistent home routine can help manage breakouts. For severe or persistent acne, see a dermatologist.

Will extractions leave marks?

Some redness after extractions is common and usually settles. Professional extractions are done carefully to reduce the risk of marks.

How often should I book an acne facial?

Your aesthetician will recommend a schedule as part of your plan.

Is The Acne Facial included in a membership?

Yes, The Acne Facial is one of the monthly facial options in the ABK GLOW CLUB agreement.

Should I stop my prescription before a facial?

Do not stop a prescription without talking to your prescriber. Tell ABK SKIN what you use when you book.
