Not sure what to book?

Start with a one-on-one consultation.

Book a consultation

Booking your first facial can feel surprisingly intimidating. You might wonder which treatment to choose, what happens in the room, whether extractions hurt, what to wear or whether you should show up with makeup on. The truth is that a good facial is relaxing, and your aesthetician will guide you through every step.

This guide walks you through a first facial at ABK SKIN in Charlotte, from booking to aftercare, so you know exactly what to expect.

In short

  • Not sure what to book? The Basic B Facial ($135, 45 min) is designed with first-time clients in mind. The Express HydraFacial ($195, 45 min) is another easy first step.
  • Every facial starts with a look at your skin, and the treatment is adjusted to what it needs that day.
  • Arrive a few minutes early and tell your aesthetician about your skincare products, medications and allergies.
  • Most facials have little or no downtime, but avoid booking anything new right before a big event.
  • Find the studio at Salon Lofts, 1133 Metropolitan Avenue in Charlotte. Entrance 1 is the door directly to the right of the Trader Joe's entrance.

How to prepare for your first facial

A few days before

  • Pause strong exfoliants and retinoids for a couple of days if you use them, unless your aesthetician tells you otherwise.
  • Avoid waxing or new treatments on your face right before your appointment.
  • Protect your skin from sunburn.

On the day

  • Come as you are. You can arrive with makeup on; your aesthetician will cleanse your skin.
  • Wear something comfortable.
  • Make a quick list of the skincare products you use, any prescription topicals or medications, allergies, and anything your skin reacts to.
  • Arrive a few minutes early so you can find the studio and settle in.

What happens during a facial, step by step

Every ABK SKIN facial is customized, so the exact steps vary. A typical first facial looks like this:

StepWhat happensWhat it feels like
1. ConversationYou talk about your skin, goals and routineRelaxed; ask anything
2. CleanseMakeup and impurities are removedGentle and soothing
3. Skin checkYour aesthetician looks closely at your skin, often under a magnifying lampBright light, nothing more
4. ExfoliationDead skin cells are lifted with an enzyme, acid or another methodLight tingling at most
5. ExtractionsClogged pores are cleared if neededBrief pressure
6. MaskA mask is chosen for your skinCool and calming
7. MassageFacial massage to relaxThe best part for many clients
8. FinishSerum, moisturizer and sunscreenFresh and hydrated

In a HydraFacial, steps 2, 4 and 5 are done with the HydraFacial device, which uses suction for painless extractions rather than squeezing.

Do extractions hurt?

Manual extractions can feel like brief pressure, and some areas are more sensitive than others. Tell your aesthetician if you want them to pause. If you are nervous about extractions, the Express HydraFacial uses painless vortex extractions.

After your first facial

Right after

Many people look fresh and glowing. Some notice mild, temporary pinkness, especially after extractions.

For the next day or two

  • Keep your routine simple and gentle.
  • Avoid scrubs, exfoliating acids and retinoids for a day or two.
  • Wear sunscreen. See the tinted sunscreen guide.
  • Do not pick at your skin.

At home

Your aesthetician may recommend products for your routine. Common starting points include a good cleanser such as Prelude Facial Treatment Cleanser and a daily sunscreen such as Intellishade Original. Read how to cleanse your face properly for the basics.

How often should you get a facial?

Many clients come in about once a month. Your skin naturally renews over a few weeks, and regular facials help you keep momentum. If you want monthly facials, a membership is usually the best value:

The takeaway

Your first facial should feel relaxing, not intimidating. At ABK SKIN in Charlotte, every facial starts with your skin and goals, and your aesthetician adjusts each step to what your skin needs that day. If you want a simple start, book The Basic B Facial or the Express HydraFacial. If you have specific concerns, start with a consultation. You can also read who ABK SKIN facials are for.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best facial for a first-timer?

The Basic B Facial is designed with first-time clients in mind. It covers exfoliation, extractions, a customized mask and a facial massage in 45 minutes for $135.

How long does a first facial take?

Most first facials at ABK SKIN take 45 to 60 minutes, depending on the treatment you book.

Should I wear makeup to my facial?

You can. Your aesthetician will cleanse your skin before starting.

Will I break out after my first facial?

Some people notice a small breakout as congestion clears, but it is not guaranteed. Keep your routine gentle, and tell your aesthetician if you are concerned.

Can I get a facial if I have sensitive skin?

Yes. Tell your aesthetician about your sensitivity, products and any reactions so they can adjust each step.

Should I get a facial before an event?

Yes, with the right timing. Avoid trying a brand-new treatment right before a big day. The event facial timing guide explains how far ahead to book.

How much is a first facial in Charlotte?

At ABK SKIN, The Basic B Facial is $135, the Express HydraFacial is $195 and most 60-minute facials are $199.

What if I cannot make my appointment?

Cancellations made less than 24 hours before an appointment are subject to a $50 charge, so cancel as early as you can.
