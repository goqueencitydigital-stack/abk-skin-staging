Booking a facial before an event: timing from the ABK menu

Which ABK SKIN treatments suit the week of an event, which need more lead time, and what to avoid beforehand.

ABKFrom the ABK SKIN studio
Charlotte, NC

In this guide
  1. Close to the date: little or no downtime
  2. One to two weeks before
  3. Plan further ahead
  4. Not sure?

Not sure what to book?

Start with a one-on-one consultation.

Book a consultation

Wedding, photos or a big night out? Timing matters as much as the treatment. This guide is based on the timing notes in ABK SKIN's own booking menu.

Close to the date: little or no downtime

  • Express HydraFacial (45 min). A deep cleanse and hydration with no downtime.
  • Platinum HydraFacial (60 min). The full HydraFacial, with lymphatic drainage, a booster and LED.
  • Dermaplaning Facial (60 min). Removes peach fuzz for smoother makeup.
  • Lymphatic Drainage Facial (60 min). For a puffy-looking face after travel.

One to two weeks before

  • ABK Hydra-Peel (90 min). Katrina's event facial: a Platinum HydraFacial plus a customized peel. The menu recommends booking one to two weeks before your event so your skin can settle.

Ready to treat your skin intentionally?

Book a one-on-one appointment in Charlotte, or start with a consultation if you are not sure which treatment fits.

Book an appointment (opens the ABK SKIN booking menu in a new tab)Start with a consultation

Plan further ahead

  • The Perfect Derma Peel. A true chemical peel. Peeling can continue for up to seven days, and you should avoid exfoliants and retinoids for a week beforehand. Leave plenty of room before your date.
  • BioRePeel Brightening Facial. Minimal visible peeling, but best results come from three to four sessions spaced 10 to 14 days apart, so start about six weeks out.
  • Microneedling. Results build over time, and redness afterwards is common. Talk through timing at a consultation.

Not sure?

Book a consultation and bring your event date. Your aesthetician can work backwards from it.
