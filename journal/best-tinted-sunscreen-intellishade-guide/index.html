The best tinted sunscreen for your skin: Intellishade Original vs Matte vs TruPhysical Clear

Compare Intellishade Original, Matte and TruPhysical Clear. Learn which tinted SPF moisturizer suits your skin, how much to apply and how to layer it.

If you only add one product to your routine, make it a daily sunscreen. Sun protection helps prevent the look of dark spots, uneven tone and early fine lines, and it protects the results of every facial, peel and microneedling session you invest in. The challenge is finding a sunscreen you actually want to wear every day.

That is why tinted sunscreen moisturizers are so popular. A good one replaces your moisturizer, evens out the look of your skin and protects in a single step. The ABK SKIN shop carries three versions of Revision Skincare's Intellishade, and this guide explains how they differ and which one suits your skin.

In short

  • Intellishade Original ($89): a 5-in-1 tinted moisturizer with broad-spectrum SPF 45, a sheer mineral tint, peptides and THD ascorbate (vitamin C).
  • Intellishade Matte ($89): inspired by the Original, with a matte finish and SPF 45. Great for oily or combination skin.
  • Intellishade TruPhysical Clear ($89): a sheer, 100% mineral, broad-spectrum SPF 50 moisturizer without a white cast.
  • Apply it last in your morning routine, and apply generously about 15 minutes before sun exposure.
  • Reapply when you are outdoors, sweating or swimming.

Why daily sunscreen matters

Sun exposure is a major driver of visible skin aging, including dark spots, uneven tone, rough texture and fine lines. It also matters if you are treating your skin. Exfoliating acids, retinoids, peels and microneedling can all make skin more sensitive to the sun, so unprotected exposure can undo your progress.

Sunscreen is not just for beach days. In Charlotte, you get sun on your daily drive, on walks around the neighborhood and through windows. Wearing it every morning is the habit that pays off most.

Intellishade Original

Intellishade Original is Revision Skincare's 5-in-1 daily anti-aging tinted moisturizer. It is formulated to correct, protect, conceal, brighten and hydrate. The sheer mineral tint adapts to give skin a more even, healthy look, and a blend of three peptides helps improve the appearance of fine lines.

Who it suits

  • Normal, dry or mature skin
  • Anyone who wants light coverage and a natural glow
  • Clients who want to simplify their morning routine

Intellishade Matte

Intellishade Matte is inspired by the Original formula, with a matte finish. It includes peptides, THD ascorbate and lecithin, which Revision says improves the appearance of pore size.

Who it suits

  • Oily or combination skin
  • Clients who get shiny by midday, especially in Charlotte summers
  • Anyone who wants a matte base under makeup

Intellishade TruPhysical Clear

Intellishade TruPhysical Clear is a sheer, 100% all-mineral, broad-spectrum SPF 50 anti-aging moisturizer. Its active ingredients are zinc oxide (15.2%) and titanium dioxide (2.5%), and it is designed to blend without a white cast or shine on all skin tones. A blend of three peptides supports the look of firmer skin.

Who it suits

  • Clients who prefer mineral-only sunscreen
  • Sensitive or post-treatment skin, when your aesthetician recommends mineral SPF
  • Anyone who wants a clear finish with no tint

How to apply tinted sunscreen

1. Apply it last in your morning skincare routine, after serums and moisturizer if you use one. 2. Use enough. Most people under-apply sunscreen. Cover your face, ears and neck evenly. 3. Apply before sun exposure. The Intellishade directions say to apply liberally 15 minutes before sun exposure. 4. Wear it alone or under makeup. 5. Reapply when you are outdoors for long periods, sweating or swimming, following the directions on the label.

How to layer vitamin C and sunscreen

Vitamin C and sunscreen work well together. Vitamin C is an antioxidant that helps defend against environmental stress, and sunscreen protects against UV. The Intellishade Matte directions note it can be used with Revision's vitamin C for added antioxidant benefits.

A simple morning routine:

1. Cleanse. 2. Apply C+ Correcting Complex 30% ($190) or use Hydri-C ($150) as your vitamin C moisturizer. 3. Finish with Intellishade.

For more detail, read the vitamin C serum guide.

Sunscreen after treatments

After a chemical peel, microneedling or dermaplaning, your skin is more vulnerable to the sun. Follow your aesthetician's aftercare instructions about when to start sunscreen and which formula to use. Many clients choose a mineral formula such as TruPhysical Clear for sensitive, freshly treated skin.

Common sunscreen mistakes

  • Only wearing it on sunny days. Wear it daily.
  • Applying too little. A thin layer gives less protection than the label suggests.
  • Forgetting your neck and ears.
  • Never reapplying during long days outdoors.
  • Relying on makeup with SPF alone. Most people do not apply enough makeup to reach the labeled protection.

The takeaway

The best tinted sunscreen is the one you will wear every day. Choose Intellishade Original for a dewy, natural glow, Intellishade Matte for oily skin and shine control, or Intellishade TruPhysical Clear for a sheer, 100% mineral formula. All three are $89 in the ABK SKIN shop, and all three help protect the results of your facials and treatments.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best tinted sunscreen for oily skin?

Intellishade Matte is designed with a matte finish, which makes it a good choice for oily or combination skin.

Is Intellishade a mineral sunscreen?

Intellishade TruPhysical Clear is 100% mineral, with zinc oxide and titanium dioxide. Intellishade Original and Matte combine mineral filters with chemical filters such as octisalate.

What SPF is Intellishade?

Intellishade Original and Matte are broad-spectrum SPF 45. Intellishade TruPhysical Clear is broad-spectrum SPF 50.

Can I wear Intellishade under makeup?

Yes. The product directions say you can wear it alone or under makeup.

Does Intellishade leave a white cast?

Intellishade TruPhysical Clear is designed to blend without a white cast on all skin tones. Original and Matte have a sheer tint.

Can I use Intellishade after a facial?

Usually, yes. After a peel, microneedling or dermaplaning, follow your aesthetician's instructions about timing and formula.

Do I need sunscreen in winter in Charlotte?

Yes. UV exposure happens year-round, including on cloudy days. Daily sunscreen protects your skin and your treatment results.

How much does Intellishade cost?

All three Intellishade options are $89 in the ABK SKIN shop.
