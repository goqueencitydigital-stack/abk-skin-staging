Dermaplaning facial in Charlotte: peach fuzz, smooth skin and what to expect

A dermaplaning facial is one of the most satisfying treatments on any facial menu. In a single appointment, the fine peach fuzz and dull surface buildup on your face are gently removed, and your skin looks brighter and feels noticeably smoother. Makeup glides on, and your serums have a cleaner surface to work on.

This guide explains what dermaplaning is, how the Dermaplaning Facial at ABK SKIN in Charlotte works, the myths about hair growing back thicker, who should wait, and how to care for your skin afterward.

In short

  • Dermaplaning uses a sterile blade to gently remove fine vellus hair (peach fuzz) and built-up dead skin cells.
  • At ABK SKIN, the Dermaplaning Facial is 60 minutes and $199, and includes LED light therapy and a personalized mask.
  • No downtime is expected, which makes it popular before events and photos.
  • Peach fuzz grows back the same. It may feel blunter as it grows, but dermaplaning does not change the hair itself.
  • Skip it for now if you have an active breakout, irritation, a sunburn or broken skin, and tell your aesthetician about any retinoids or recent peels.

What is dermaplaning?

Dermaplaning is a manual exfoliation technique. A licensed aesthetician holds a sterile, surgical-style blade at a shallow angle and uses short, gentle strokes across the face. The blade lifts two things at once:

1. Vellus hair, the soft, fine "peach fuzz" that covers most of the face. 2. Dead skin cells that sit on the surface and make skin look dull or feel rough.

The result is a smooth, bright surface. Many people are surprised by how much comes off, which is part of the appeal.

Dermaplaning benefits

Smoother, softer skin

Removing peach fuzz and surface buildup leaves skin that feels noticeably smoother to the touch.

A brighter look

When dead skin cells are removed, light reflects more evenly from the surface, which gives a fresher, brighter look.

Better makeup application

Foundation and tinted sunscreen can catch on peach fuzz and dry patches. After dermaplaning, makeup tends to sit more smoothly, which is why many clients book it before events.

A better base for your skincare

With less buildup on top, your serums and moisturizers spread more evenly across a clean surface.

Does dermaplaning make hair grow back thicker?

No. This is the most common question about dermaplaning. Removing vellus hair at the surface does not change the hair follicle, so the hair grows back with the same color and texture. Because the blade cuts the hair straight across, regrowth can feel a little blunt at first. That is temporary, and it is not thicker hair.

Dermaplaning is designed for fine vellus hair. If you have coarser facial hair or hair growth that has changed suddenly, talk to your doctor, because that can be worth looking into.

Who dermaplaning suits, and who should wait

Good candidates

  • Dull, rough-looking skin
  • Visible peach fuzz that catches makeup
  • Clients who want an instant glow before photos or events
  • Clients who want exfoliation without an acid peel

When to wait or ask first

Tell your aesthetician before your appointment if any of these apply:

  • An active breakout, especially inflamed or cystic acne. Book The Acne Facial instead, and read why acne needs a plan.
  • A sunburn, cuts, rashes or irritated skin.
  • Retinoids, exfoliating acids or prescription topicals in your routine.
  • A recent peel, microneedling or other resurfacing treatment.
  • A history of cold sores, skin conditions or sensitivity.

Your aesthetician can adjust or reschedule if dermaplaning is not right for your skin that day.

How often should you get dermaplaning?

Your skin naturally sheds and renews, and peach fuzz grows back over a few weeks. Many people book dermaplaning about once a month. Your aesthetician can recommend a schedule based on your skin's sensitivity and your other treatments. If you want monthly facials, the Dermaplaning Facial is one of the monthly options in ABK GLOW CLUB, which is $149 a month with a 3-month minimum.

Dermaplaning before an event

Because no downtime is expected, dermaplaning is a favorite for weddings, photos and big nights out. A common approach is to book a few days before the event, so any temporary sensitivity has settled and your makeup goes on smoothly. For a full timeline, read when to book a facial before an event.

Can you dermaplane at home?

At-home facial razors are widely available, but a professional dermaplaning facial is different. A licensed aesthetician uses a sterile blade, checks your skin first, holds the skin taut, uses the right angle and follows up with LED and a mask chosen for you. If your skin is sensitive, acne-prone or on active ingredients, a professional appointment is the safer place to start.

The takeaway

A dermaplaning facial removes peach fuzz and dull surface buildup in one appointment, with no downtime expected. It leaves skin smoother and brighter and gives makeup a smoother base. Frequently asked questions

How much is dermaplaning in Charlotte?

The Dermaplaning Facial at ABK SKIN is $199 for about 60 minutes, and it includes LED light therapy and a personalized face mask.

Does dermaplaning hurt?

Most people find it comfortable. It feels like light, gentle strokes across the skin.

Will my hair grow back thicker or darker after dermaplaning?

No. Dermaplaning removes vellus hair at the surface and does not change the follicle. Regrowth can feel blunt for a short time, but the hair is the same.

Is there downtime after dermaplaning?

None is expected. Some people notice mild, temporary pinkness, especially with sensitive skin.

Can I wear makeup after dermaplaning?

Many people wear makeup the next day. Ask your aesthetician what suits your skin, and keep the first day as simple as you can.

Can I get dermaplaning if I have acne?

Not during an active, inflamed breakout. The Acne Facial is a better choice until your skin calms down.

How often should I get dermaplaning?

Many clients book about once a month. Your aesthetician can tailor the schedule to your skin.

Is dermaplaning included in a membership?

The Dermaplaning Facial is one of the monthly options in ABK GLOW CLUB. The Express HydraFacial option in ABK SKIN CLUB includes dermaplaning.
