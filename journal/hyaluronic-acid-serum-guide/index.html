Hyaluronic acid serum: how to use it, and Renewing vs Restorative HA

What hyaluronic acid serum does for dehydrated skin, how to apply it, and how Hydrinity's Renewing HA, Restorative HA and HA+ Masque differ.

If your skin feels tight after cleansing, looks dull by mid-afternoon or shows fine lines that seem deeper on dry days, your skin may be dehydrated. A hyaluronic acid serum is one of the simplest ways to help. It is lightweight, suits nearly every skin type and layers easily into almost any routine.

This guide explains what hyaluronic acid does, how to use an HA serum properly, and the difference between the Hydrinity hyaluronic acid products in the ABK SKIN shop: Renewing HA Serum, Restorative HA Serum and Restorative HA+ Masque.

In short

  • Hyaluronic acid (HA) is a humectant that attracts and holds water, helping skin look plumper and more hydrated.
  • Apply it to clean, slightly damp skin and follow with a moisturizer to seal in hydration.
  • Renewing HA Serum ($140, travel size $80): for everyday hydration plus visible signs of aging.
  • Restorative HA Serum ($120, travel size $72): for sensitive, compromised or freshly treated skin.
  • Restorative HA+ Masque ($120, box of 5): a biocellulose sheet mask for a hydration boost.

What does hyaluronic acid do?

Hyaluronic acid occurs naturally in the skin. In skincare, it acts as a humectant, which means it draws water in and helps hold it there. The result is skin that looks plumper, smoother and more radiant, and feels more comfortable.

HA is useful for:

  • Dehydrated, tight-feeling skin
  • Dullness
  • The look of fine lines caused by dehydration
  • Comfort while using retinoids or exfoliating acids
  • Recovery after professional treatments, when recommended by your aesthetician

Dry skin vs dehydrated skin

These terms are often used interchangeably, but they are different.

Dry skinDehydrated skin
What it lacksOilWater
Can it be oily too?RarelyYes, oily skin can be dehydrated
SignsFlaking, rough patches, feels dry all the timeTightness, dullness, fine lines more visible, makeup sits on top
What helpsRicher moisturizers and oilsHumectants such as hyaluronic acid, plus a moisturizer

Many people have both. A hyaluronic acid serum addresses the water side, and a moisturizer addresses the oil and barrier side. Learn more on the dull and dehydrated skin page.

Renewing HA Serum

Renewing HA Serum with PPM⁶ Technology is Hydrinity's super-hydrating serum designed to reduce the signs of skin aging. It is powered by patented Supercharged Hyaluronic Acid, and Hydrinity says it helps attract moisture, boost radiance and improve elasticity. It is non-greasy, non-comedogenic and dermatologist tested.

Choose Renewing HA if your skin is generally healthy and you want daily hydration with anti-aging benefits. The travel size ($80) is a smaller way to try it.

Restorative HA Serum

Restorative HA Serum with PPM⁶ Technology pairs Supercharged Hyaluronic Acid with peptides and antioxidants. Hydrinity designed it to enhance hydration and luminosity while helping delicate, compromised skin recover. It is gentle and soothing.

Choose Restorative HA if your skin is sensitive, irritated, over-exfoliated or recovering from a professional treatment. The travel size is $72.

Restorative HA+ Masque

The Restorative HA+ Masque is a biocellulose sheet mask that delivers deeply penetrating hydration with calming, smoothing effects. Use it when your skin needs a boost: after travel, during dry weather or before an event.

How to use hyaluronic acid serum

1. Cleanse with a gentle cleanser such as Prelude Facial Treatment Cleanser. 2. Leave skin slightly damp, or mist with Hyacyn Active. HA works best with water to hold onto. 3. Apply a few drops of HA serum and press it into your face and neck. 4. Layer treatment serums such as vitamin C in the morning or retinoids at night, as your routine calls for. 5. Seal it in with a moisturizer. This helps keep the hydration from evaporating. 6. Finish with sunscreen in the morning. See the tinted sunscreen guide.

Hyaluronic acid after treatments

After microneedling, a chemical peel or other professional treatments, your aesthetician may recommend soothing hydration. The Restorative Post Procedure Kit ($160) pairs Hyacyn Active with Restorative HA Serum for this purpose. Always follow your aftercare instructions first.

If you want hydration delivered during treatment, the hydration option of Enhanced Microneedling and the Glass Skin Facial are both built around dewy, hydrated skin.

Common hyaluronic acid mistakes

  • Applying it to bone-dry skin in a very dry room, then skipping moisturizer
  • Using too much. A few drops are enough.
  • Expecting it to replace moisturizer. HA attracts water; a moisturizer helps hold it in.
  • Ignoring your barrier. If your skin stings, read the over-exfoliation guide.

The takeaway

Hyaluronic acid is one of the easiest ingredients to add to your routine. It hydrates, plumps the look of skin and makes actives easier to tolerate. Frequently asked questions

What does hyaluronic acid serum do?

It attracts and holds water in the skin, helping it look plumper, smoother and more radiant.

Should I use hyaluronic acid in the morning or at night?

Both. Hyaluronic acid works well morning and evening, under moisturizer and, in the morning, under sunscreen.

What is the difference between Renewing HA and Restorative HA?

Renewing HA is designed for everyday hydration and visible signs of aging. Restorative HA adds peptides and antioxidants and is designed for sensitive, compromised or recovering skin.

Can I use hyaluronic acid with retinol?

Yes. Many people use HA to help keep skin comfortable while using retinoids.

Can I use hyaluronic acid with vitamin C?

Yes. Apply vitamin C, then your HA serum, then moisturizer and sunscreen in the morning.

Is hyaluronic acid good for oily skin?

Yes. Oily skin can still be dehydrated, and both Hydrinity HA serums are non-comedogenic.

Can I use hyaluronic acid after microneedling?

Your aesthetician may recommend a soothing hydrating serum after microneedling. Follow their aftercare instructions first.

Is there a travel size?

Yes. Renewing HA Serum comes in an $80 travel size and Restorative HA Serum in a $72 travel size.
