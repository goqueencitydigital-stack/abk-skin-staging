Questions

How much is an ABK SKIN gift card?

The ABK eGift Card is $200. It is the only amount currently offered online.

How is the gift card delivered?

By email. At checkout you enter your recipient’s name and email, and they receive an email with their gift card code. You can send it straight away or schedule it for a later date.

Can I add a personal message?

Yes. There is space for an optional personal message at checkout, up to 1,000 characters.

How long is the gift card valid?

The checkout shows the eGift Card as valid for one year.

What can it be used for?

Eligible ABK SKIN services and skincare products. If the full amount is not used in one visit, the remaining balance can go toward a future eligible purchase.

How does my recipient use it?

They book their appointment as usual, then use their gift card code to pay at checkout. The card needs to be available at the time of the appointment or purchase.

Can it be refunded or exchanged for cash?

No. Gift card purchases are final and non-refundable, and cards have no cash value, except where required by law. A card can be gifted or transferred to someone else.
