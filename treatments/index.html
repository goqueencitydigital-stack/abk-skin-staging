Questions

Before you book

How do I book a treatment?

Use any Book button to open the ABK SKIN booking menu. Choose the category, then the treatment, and pick a time that suits you.

I am not sure which treatment to book. Where should I start?

Start with a skin consultation. You will talk through your goals, get a detailed skin analysis and leave with a plan built around your skin.

Are appointments one-on-one?

Yes. ABK SKIN is a boutique studio, and every appointment is one-on-one.

What is the cancellation policy?

Appointments cancelled less than 24 hours in advance are charged $50.

Do you offer memberships?

Yes. ABK GLOW CLUB includes one facial a month and ABK SKIN CLUB includes one premium treatment a month. See the Memberships page for details.

Where is the studio?

1133 Metropolitan Avenue, Suite 150, Loft 2, inside Salon Lofts, Charlotte, NC 28204.
