About this treatment

There is no fixed routine in the ABK Glow Facial. Your aesthetician takes the lead and builds each step around your skin goals and how your skin looks when you arrive.

Because your skin changes, every session is a little different. You lie back and relax while the treatment is curated around you.

Inside an ABK Glow Facial

Questions

ABK Glow Facial, answered.

How long does the ABK Glow Facial take?

Plan for about 90 minutes. Every appointment at ABK SKIN is one-on-one.

How much does the ABK Glow Facial cost in Charlotte?

The ABK Glow Facial is $325 at ABK SKIN. It is also part of ABK SKIN CLUB, if you would like to book it monthly.

Is there any downtime after the ABK Glow Facial?

Depends on the steps chosen; usually none.

Who is the ABK Glow Facial good for?

It is often booked for clients who want the treatment chosen for them; a longer, more relaxing appointment; and changing or mixed skin needs. Not sure it is right for you? A consultation is a good place to start.

Where can I get the ABK Glow Facial in Charlotte?

At ABK SKIN, a private one-on-one skin studio at 1133 Metropolitan Avenue, Suite 150, Loft 2, inside Salon Lofts, Charlotte, NC 28204. Visits are by appointment.
