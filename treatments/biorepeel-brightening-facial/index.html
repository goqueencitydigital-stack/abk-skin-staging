About this treatment

BioRePeel exfoliates and renews the skin's surface without the prolonged peeling of a traditional chemical peel. It can help improve the appearance of dullness, uneven tone, rough texture, acne and post-acne marks, and fine lines.

For best results, ABK SKIN recommends a series of three to four treatments spaced 10 to 14 days apart, followed by a maintenance plan built around your skin.

How it works

Two phases, one treatment

BioRePeel is built on biphasic technology. Each vial holds two phases that stay separate until your treatment, then are mixed right before they touch your skin.

The lipophilic phase

Helps protect and support your skin’s natural oils, and helps hold in moisture while the active ingredients go to work.

The hydrophilic phase

Carries the exfoliating acids, amino acids and vitamins that renew the surface of your skin for a brighter, smoother look.
