Microneedling

Enhanced Microneedling

Microneedling paired with a professional serum delivered through the micro-channels. Four options, each about 90 minutes.

Length
1 hr 30 min
Price
$550
Downtime
Ask at your consultation; redness after microneedling is common.
See the four options

About this treatment

Each option below is a separate appointment in the ABK SKIN booking menu. They share the same microneedling base and differ in the serum used during treatment.

Not sure which one fits? Book a consultation, or book standard Microneedling and ask about upgrading.

Choose your option

Microneedling + GHK-Cu + PDRN

1 hr 30 min$550

Uses BioSkin NEXTGEN PDRN+, which combines PDRN (a DNA-derived ingredient, the treatment the old website called the salmon sperm facial), GHK-Cu copper peptide and hyaluronic acid. Chosen for texture, firmness, tone and overall skin quality.

In the booking menu, choose Microneedling, then Microneedling + GHK-Cu + PDRN.

Book this option (opens the ABK SKIN booking menu in a new tab)

Microneedling + Mesotherapy Firming

1 hr 30 min$550

Pairs microneedling with a firming mesotherapy serum. Chosen for skin starting to lose firmness, fine lines, texture and enlarged pores.

In the booking menu, choose Microneedling, then Microneedling + Mesotherapy Firming.

Book this option (opens the ABK SKIN booking menu in a new tab)

Microneedling + Radiance Mesotherapy

1 hr 30 min$550

Pairs microneedling with Radiance serum mesotherapy. Chosen for dullness, uneven tone, dehydration and texture.

In the booking menu, choose Microneedling, then Microneedling + Radiance Mesotherapy.

Book this option (opens the ABK SKIN booking menu in a new tab)

Microneedling + Hydrate Mesotherapy

1 hr 30 min$550

Pairs microneedling with BioSkin hydrating mesotherapy. Chosen for skin that feels dehydrated, dull or tight.

In the booking menu, choose Microneedling, then Microneedling + Hydrate Mesotherapy.

Book this option (opens the ABK SKIN booking menu in a new tab)

Often booked for

  • Clients already considering microneedling who want a targeted serum
  • Texture, firmness, tone or dehydration goals
