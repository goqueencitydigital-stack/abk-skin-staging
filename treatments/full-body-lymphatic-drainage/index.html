About this treatment

Full Body Lymphatic Drainage uses slow, intentional techniques on the areas where you feel puffy, heavy or stagnant. It is a deeply relaxing reset after travel, before an event or when your body needs a break.

If you only want drainage on the face, the Lymphatic Drainage Facial may be the better booking.

What’s included

  • Lymphatic drainage massage
  • Areas available: face, arms, legs, abdomen, back

Often booked for

  • Post-travel puffiness
  • Feeling heavy or sluggish
  • Pre-event preparation
  • Relaxation

Good to know: Tell your aesthetician about any medical conditions before booking. Lymphatic massage is a wellness and appearance service, not a medical treatment.
