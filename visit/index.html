Getting here

Three steps to Loft 2

  1. 01

    Head to 1133 Metropolitan Avenue

    Set your map to the address above. The studio is in the same building as Trader Joe’s.

  2. 02

    Use Entrance 1

    Entrance 1 is the door directly to the right of the Trader Joe's entrance. Look for the Salon Lofts glass doors marked 150.

  3. 03

    Find Loft 2

    Once you’re inside Salon Lofts, ABK SKIN is Loft 2. Katrina will be ready for you at your appointment time.
