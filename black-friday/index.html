Questions

Black Friday FAQ

When do the Black Friday deals end?

All Black Friday deals run through Monday, November 30.

Do I need a code for 25% off skincare?

No. The 25% comes off automatically in your cart on every skincare product in the ABK shop. Services and gift cards are not included.

How do I claim a treatment offer?

Buy a three-treatment package on this page and check out securely. You pay for two treatments and get the third free. Prefer to call? Ring (980) 408-9672 and the ABK SKIN team will set it up for you.

How do I book after I buy a package?

Right after checkout, you will see a page with a button to book your first visit. In the booking notes, write Black Friday package. Your confirmation email has your order number and a download with your booking steps, so you can book your other two visits the same way.

Which Enhanced Microneedling options count?

All four Enhanced Microneedling options are included in the buy 2, get 1 free offer.

Are the bundle products full size?

Yes. Each bundle includes three full-size products at 30% off what they cost separately.
