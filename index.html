Welcome to ABK SKIN

Your trusted skin studio in Charlotte, NC

ABK SKIN is a boutique, one-on-one skincare studio in the heart of Charlotte, NC, where every service is delivered by me, your dedicated solo aesthetician, Katrina. The ABK Methodology

A scientific approach to beautiful skin

We follow a 4-step process to build a personalized treatment plan around your long-term skin goals.

  1. Analyze

    A detailed skin analysis of your barrier, texture, tone and current routine.

    01

  2. Correct

    Targeted treatments such as chemical peels and HydraFacials for the concerns you want to work on.

    02

  3. Build

    Collagen-focused treatments, including microneedling with PDRN and GHK-Cu.

    03

  4. Maintain

    A customized home-care routine paired with regular maintenance treatments.

    04
