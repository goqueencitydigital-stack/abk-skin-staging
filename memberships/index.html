Your monthly menu

What you can book each month

ABK SKIN CLUB · $199/month

Choose one premium treatment each month

Booked one at a time, the ABK Glow Facial is listed at $325 and the BioRePeel Brightening Facial at $295. As a member, you pay $199 a month.

  • ABK Glow Facial

    A 90-minute custom facial that follows what your skin is asking for that day.

  • Platinum HydraFacial

    The most complete HydraFacial at ABK SKIN, with lymphatic drainage, a customized booster and LED light therapy.

  • Express HydraFacial with dermaplaning

    The shorter HydraFacial: a deep cleanse, gentle acid exfoliation, painless extractions and hydration, in about 45 minutes.

  • BioRePeel Brightening Facial

    A peel-style treatment for brighter, smoother-looking skin, designed to keep visible peeling to a minimum.

Monthly options as listed in the ABK SKIN CLUB agreement. The Express HydraFacial option includes dermaplaning. Prefer not to choose? Leave it to your aesthetician.

ABK GLOW CLUB · $149/month

Choose one facial each month

Booked one at a time, these facials are listed at $175–$195 each. As a member, you pay $149 a month.

  • Express HydraFacial

    The shorter HydraFacial: a deep cleanse, gentle acid exfoliation, painless extractions and hydration, in about 45 minutes.

  • Glass Skin Facial

    A fully customized facial aimed at smooth, hydrated skin with a lit-from-within look.

  • Dermaplaning Facial

    Dermaplaning to remove peach fuzz and dull surface buildup, finished with LED light and a mask chosen for your skin.

  • The Sculpting Facial

    Intraoral buccal massage, jaw tension release and lymphatic techniques for a face that feels lighter and looks more defined.

  • The Acne Facial

    A facial for breakouts and congestion that changes with your skin, plus a plan for what to do at home.

Monthly facial options as listed in the ABK GLOW CLUB agreement. Prefer not to choose? Leave it to your aesthetician.

How it works

Three steps to joining

  1. 01

    Choose your club

    ABK SKIN CLUB for premium treatments like the Platinum HydraFacial and BioRePeel, or ABK GLOW CLUB for one facial a month.

  2. 02

    Join online

    Review and accept the membership agreement in the ABK SKIN booking portal. There is no setup fee.

  3. 03

    Book every month

    Pick your treatment from your club’s menu, or leave the choice to your aesthetician, and use your skincare discount.

Six-month anniversary

Stay six months and your treatment gets an upgrade.

GLOW members can upgrade to a Platinum HydraFacial or a chemical peel. SKIN members can upgrade to a qualifying advanced treatment, as set out in the membership agreement.

Questions

Membership FAQ

Is there a minimum commitment?

Yes. Both memberships have a three-month minimum commitment.

Can I let my aesthetician choose my treatment?

Yes. Each month you can choose from the eligible treatments or leave the choice to your aesthetician based on what your skin needs.

Does the skincare discount apply in the online shop?

The agreements describe 10% (GLOW) and 20% (SKIN) off ABK SKIN skincare. Ask how to apply your member discount to online orders.

Is there a setup fee?

No. Neither membership has a setup fee.

How do I join?

Use any Join button on this page. It opens the membership agreement in the ABK SKIN booking portal, which is the governing document for benefits, eligible treatments and cancellation.

Which membership is right for me?

ABK GLOW CLUB suits clients who want one facial every month. ABK SKIN CLUB is for clients who want premium treatments such as the Platinum HydraFacial or the BioRePeel Brightening Facial. If you are unsure, book a consultation first.
