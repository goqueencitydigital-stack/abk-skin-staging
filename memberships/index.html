Questions

Membership FAQ

Is there a minimum commitment?

Yes. Both memberships have a three-month minimum commitment.

Can I let my aesthetician choose my treatment?

Yes. Each month you can choose from the eligible treatments or leave the choice to your aesthetician based on what your skin needs.

Does the skincare discount apply in the online shop?

The agreements describe 10% (GLOW) and 20% (SKIN) off ABK SKIN skincare. Ask how to apply your member discount to online orders.
