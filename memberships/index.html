Questions

Membership FAQ

Is there a minimum commitment?

Yes. Both memberships have a three-month minimum commitment.

Can I let my aesthetician choose my treatment?

Yes. Each month you can choose from the eligible treatments or leave the choice to your aesthetician based on what your skin needs.

Does the skincare discount apply in the online shop?

The agreements describe 10% (GLOW) and 20% (SKIN) off ABK SKIN skincare. Ask how to apply your member discount to online orders.

Is there a setup fee?

No. Neither membership has a setup fee.

How do I join?

Use any Join button on this page. It opens the membership agreement in the ABK SKIN booking portal, which is the governing document for benefits, eligible treatments and cancellation.

Which membership is right for me?

ABK GLOW CLUB suits clients who want one facial every month. ABK SKIN CLUB is for clients who want premium treatments such as the Platinum HydraFacial or the BioRePeel Brightening Facial. If you are unsure, book a consultation first.
