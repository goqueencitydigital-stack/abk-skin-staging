How it works

Five simple steps

  1. 1

    Choose your appointment

    Browse treatments or skin concerns. If you are unsure, book a consultation.

  2. 2

    Book online

    Every Book button opens the ABK SKIN booking menu. Pick the category and service by name, then a time.

  3. 3

    Complete your details

    The booking menu asks for your details and payment information to hold the appointment.

  4. 4

    Find the studio

    Salon Lofts at 1133 Metropolitan Avenue. Entrance 1 is the door directly to the right of the Trader Joe's entrance.

  5. 5

    Your appointment

    Your skin is assessed and the treatment adjusted to what it needs that day.

Good to know

Policies

  • Cancellations: Cancellations made less than 24 hours before an appointment are subject to a $50 charge.
  • Paying over time: The booking menu shows Affirm as an option at checkout.
  • Products: All product sales are final.
