Details

Firming Night Treatment is a peptide-rich, age-defying moisturizer designed to deeply hydrate, replenish, and visibly firm the skin while you sleep. This luxurious overnight cream delivers both immediate and long-term moisturization, helping reduce the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles while brightening dull, dry, fatigued skin. Powered by clinically proven peptides, Sodium Hyaluronate, Shea Butter, and nourishing botanical extracts, it restores suppleness and revitalizes the complexion for smoother, more radiant skin by morning.

Key Benefits

Reduces the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles with clinically proven peptides

Provides deep, sustained hydration with moisture-binding Sodium Hyaluronate

Alleviates dryness with Shea Butter and nourishing botanical extracts

Replenishes and brightens dull, dry skin for a more radiant morning glow

Supports overnight skin recovery to improve texture and suppleness
