Hydrogenated Polyisobutene, Jojoba Esters, Phenyl Trimethicone, Tribehenin, Helianthus Annuus (Sunflower) Seed Oil Unsaponifiables, Ethylhexyl Palmitate, Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea) Butter, Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea) Butter Unsaponifiables, Sodium Hyaluronate, Sucrose Cocoate, Tetrahexyldecyl Ascorbate, Tocopheryl Acetate, Camellia Sinensis (Green Tea) Leaf Extract, Palmitoyl Tripeptide-1, Palmitoyl Tripeptide-38, Dipalmitoyl Hydroxyproline, Portulaca Pilosa Extract, Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride, Butylene Glycol, Cetearyl Ethylhexanoate, Sorbitan Isostearate, Silica Dimethyl Silylate, Glyceryl Caprylate, Mentha Arvensis Leaf Oil, Red 27 (CI 45410), Pentylene Glycol, Tocopherol, Lactic Acid, Limonene, Menthol, Pinene, Terpineol

How-To-Use

Glide onto lips in a massaging motion. Use three times daily for optimal results or as needed. Use alone or layered over your favorite lip color.

Application Tips

1. Use over your lip color for a glossy look that lasts.

2. Easily clean ceramic tip with a cleansing wipe to remove excess product.

3. Apply a thinner layer for a lower gloss look.
