Details

Nectifirm® Advanced is an award-winning, next-generation neck treatment formulated to address the moderate to advanced visible signs of aging on the neck and décolletage. This groundbreaking formula harnesses the power of Microbiome technology, eight distinctive peptides, and Smart Antioxidant Technology to support the skin’s natural repair processes and improve firmness, lift, brightness, and texture. With consistent use, the appearance of adipose tissue, crepey skin, fine lines, and wrinkles is noticeably reduced, revealing a smoother, more refined, and dramatically more youthful-looking neck.

Key Benefits

Targets moderate to advanced visible signs of neck and décolletage aging

Powered by industry-leading Microbiome technology to support skin resilience

Features eight distinctive peptides to visibly firm and lift

Smart Antioxidant Technology helps boost the skin’s own antioxidant system

Reduces the appearance of adipose tissue for a more refined contour

Visibly improves crepey skin, rough texture, fine lines, and wrinkles

Enhances brightness and overall skin quality for a more youthful look

How-To-Use

Dispense 2 pumps into palm of hand. Using upward strokes, gently apply onto décolletage, working your way up the neck to the jawline. Use twice daily
