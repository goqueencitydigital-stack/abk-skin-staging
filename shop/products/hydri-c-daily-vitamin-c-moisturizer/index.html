Key Ingredients

Tetrahexyldecyl Ascorbate

A highly stable, oil-soluble form of vitamin C known for its antioxidant properties, ability to brighten and even skin tone, and support collagen production.

Kakadu Plum Extract

This native Australian plant rich in skin rejuvenating and repairing vitamin C, helps fight free radicals.

Green Tea Extract

Loaded with antioxidant properties, this potent extract helps increase cutaneous microcirculation, activates cellular renewal, and helps protect the skin from environmental stressors.

Jojoba Seed Oil

Extracted from the Jojoba plant, this soothing oil closely matches our own natural sebum. As a rich source of fatty acids and antioxidants, it enhances skin’s restorative properties and provides skin soothing benefits.

3rd Generation Squalane

A lightweight, non-comedogenic oil that effectively hydrates and balances the skin’s moisture levels while also helping to improve skin texture and appearance.

Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea Butter) Oil

A rich emollient that forms a nourishing barrier to help reduce water loss (TEWL). Contains high levels of vitamins and fatty acids to help improve elasticity and restore dry, dehydrated skin.

Description

MicroFusion Technology uses a patented molecule to penetrate deep into the dermal layer delivering a potent but gentle form of Vitamin C along with other powerful actives and is suitable for all skin types. In as little as 2-weeks, HYDRI-C provides powerful antioxidant protection, helps stimulate collagen production, reduces wrinkles, brightens skin, improves elasticity and helps to reduce blemishes.

Complete Ingredients

Water/Aqua/Eau, Tetrahexyldecyl Ascorbate, Squalane, Polyglyceryl-6 Distearate, Glycerin, Stearyl Alcohol, Fulvic Acid, Raphanus Sativus (Radish) Root Extract, Cetyl Alcohol, Jojoba Esters, Butyrospermum Parkii Oil, Dimethicone, Simmondsia Chinensis (Jojoba) Seed Extract, Bisabolol, Pullulan, Sodium Hyaluronate, Camellia Sinensis Leaf Extract, Terminalia Ferdinandiana Fruit Extract, Ethylhexylglycerin, Tocopherol, Helianthus Annuus Oil, Sodium Acrylates Copolymer, Polyglyceryl-3 Beeswax, Lecithin, Saccharide Isomerate, Xanthan Gum, Limonene, Sclerotium Gum, Phenoxyethanol, Silica, Sodium Citrate, Citric Acid, Potassium Sorbate, Sodium Benzoate, Myrcene.

Application

After cleansing, apply 1 to 2 pumps to the face, neck, and décolleté morning and evening. Apply before sunscreen in the morning.
