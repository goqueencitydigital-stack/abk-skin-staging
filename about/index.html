The team

Founder · Licensed aesthetician

Katrina

  • Founderof ABK SKIN
  • Age 19when her aesthetics journey began
  • Acneand scarring specialist

Katrina created ABK SKIN as a boutique, one-on-one studio where clients are never treated like a number in the door. You get her full attention from the moment you arrive, including a detailed skin analysis and a customized treatment plan built around your skin goals. With the help of her medical director, she pairs medical-grade facials with medical-grade skincare to transform skin.

She specializes in acne, scarring, pigmentation, fine lines and wrinkles, damaged skin barriers and long-term skin health, with a focus on preventive skincare science. Some of her most loved services are the Salmon Sperm Facial, custom Platinum HydraFacials, and full-body lymphatic drainage.

Her passion is personal. Katrina struggled with cystic acne in her early adulthood, and it took a real toll on her confidence. At 19, her first aesthetic appointment at a plastic surgeon’s office showed her what skin treatments could really do, and she knew she wanted to help others overcome their own insecurities someday.

Her goal: to keep you with her for a lifetime, so you never need aggressive resurfacing treatments.

Master Medical Aesthetician

Mckensi

  • 12 yearsin aesthetics
  • Honorsgraduate of the Paul Mitchell School
  • Favoritetreatment: microneedling

With 12 years of experience in aesthetics, Mckensi is a Master Medical Aesthetician who is passionate about helping people feel confident and comfortable in their skin. She graduated with honors from the Paul Mitchell School and has built her career around combining advanced treatments with personalized skincare.

Microneedling is her favorite treatment, especially for its ability to support collagen production and overall skin rejuvenation. She is also a self-proclaimed retinol enthusiast and a huge HydraFacial fan, because great skin is all about consistency, the right treatments and a little bit of skincare obsession.

Outside of aesthetics, Mckensi is a dog mom to a Black Lab and a Doberman and loves spending time with her boyfriend, Jordan. She is always learning, always talking about skincare, and genuinely loves getting to know the people she has the privilege of treating.

Her goal is simple: to create an experience where every client feels heard, cared for and excited about their skin.

The ABK Method

Analyze. Correct. Build. Maintain.

How a plan at ABK SKIN usually comes together.

  1. 01

    Analyze

    A detailed look at your skin, your routine and your goals, at a consultation or the start of your first visit.

  2. 02

    Correct

    Treatments chosen for what you want to change, such as peels, microneedling or a corrective facial.

  3. 03

    Build

    A home routine with medical-grade skincare so results continue between appointments.

  4. 04

    Maintain

    Regular appointments, often through a membership, to keep skin on track as it changes.

Review: Only the four words of the ABK Method appear on the current site. The step descriptions are drafted from the About page and need Katrina’s approval.
