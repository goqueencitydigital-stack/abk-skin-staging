About ABK SKIN

Meet Katrina

ABK SKIN is a boutique, one-on-one skincare studio in Charlotte, founded by Katrina, a licensed aesthetician.

Review: About page says every service is delivered by Katrina as a solo aesthetician. The booking portal also lists McKensi West, Licensed Aesthetician. Confirm how the team should be described.

In Katrina’s words

“I created ABK SKIN to offer a more personal, intentional, and results-driven experience where my clients aren’t looked at as a dollar sign or number in the door.

Here, you receive my full attention from the moment you arrive, including detailed skin analysis and customized treatment plans designed around your skin goals. With the help of my medical director, I pair medical-grade facials and skincare to transform skin.”

Where it started

Katrina struggled with cystic acne in early adulthood, and it affected her confidence. Her own journey in aesthetics began at 19, at her first appointment in a plastic surgeon’s office, where she saw what skin treatments could do.

Seeing how acne had affected her mental health deepened her wish to help others, and she set out to create a welcoming space where clients feel cared for.

What she focuses on

  • Acne and scarring
  • Pigmentation
  • Fine lines and wrinkles
  • Damaged skin barriers
  • Long-term skin health

Her approach is preventive. The goal is to keep clients for a lifetime and help them avoid needing aggressive resurfacing later.

The ABK Method

Analyze. Correct. Build. Maintain.

How a plan at ABK SKIN usually comes together.

  1. 01

    Analyze

    A detailed look at your skin, your routine and your goals, at a consultation or the start of your first visit.

  2. 02

    Correct

    Treatments chosen for what you want to change, such as peels, microneedling or a corrective facial.

  3. 03

    Build

    A home routine with medical-grade skincare so results continue between appointments.

  4. 04

    Maintain

    Regular appointments, often through a membership, to keep skin on track as it changes.

Review: Only the four words of the ABK Method appear on the current site. The step descriptions are drafted from the About page and need Katrina’s approval.
