FAQ

Questions, answered.

Booking, your visit, memberships and the shop.

Booking

How do I book an appointment?

Use any Book button on this site. It opens the ABK SKIN booking menu, run on Aesthetic Record. Choose the category, then the service by name, pick a time and confirm your details.

Why doesn't the Book button open the exact treatment?

The booking menu does not support links to individual services yet, so every Book button opens the same menu. Each treatment page tells you which category and service name to choose.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellations made less than 24 hours before your appointment are subject to a $50 charge.

Can I pay over time?

The booking portal shows Affirm as a pay-over-time option at checkout. Availability and terms are set by Affirm and shown when you book.

Where do I see prices?

Prices are shown on each treatment page where the booking menu and Katrina's published menu agree. Where they differ or no price is listed, the page says View current pricing and links to the booking menu.

Your visit

Where is ABK SKIN?

1133 Metropolitan Avenue, Suite 150, Loft 2 inside Salon Lofts, Charlotte, NC 28204. Entrance 1 is the door directly to the right of the Trader Joe's entrance.

What are your hours?

ABK SKIN is by appointment. The open times in the booking menu are the most accurate view of availability.

Who will I see?

ABK SKIN is a boutique studio founded by Katrina, a licensed aesthetician who works with a medical director.

Review: The booking portal also lists McKensi West, Licensed Aesthetician, while the About page says Katrina is a solo aesthetician. Confirm who should be named.
How do I log in to my client account?

Use the Client Portal link in the menu. It opens ABK SKIN's Aesthetic Record client portal, where you sign in with your email.

Memberships and gift cards

How do memberships work?

There are two monthly memberships, ABK GLOW CLUB at $149 a month and ABK SKIN CLUB at $199 a month. Both have a three-month minimum and no setup fee. The membership agreement in the booking portal sets the full terms.

Which gift card should I buy?

ABK SKIN currently sells gift cards in two places: the ABK SKIN shop and the booking portal. Each has its own amounts and terms, shown on our Gift Cards page.

Shop

How do I buy skincare?

Each product page has a button that opens the same product in the ABK SKIN shop, where you add it to your cart and check out. This site does not keep its own cart.

Do you ship?

The shop ships within the United States. Orders are processed and shipped within 3 to 5 business days, and shipping is calculated at checkout.

Can I return a product?

All product sales are final.
