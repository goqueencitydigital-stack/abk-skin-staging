Consultations · Virtual

Can’t make it to the studio? Meet virtually.

The same one-on-one consultation, by phone or video, from wherever you are. You will break down what is going on with your skin, look at the products you use now, talk through your lifestyle and leave with a plan built for you.

Length
1 hr
Format
Phone or video
Price

In your consultation

Three conversations, one plan.

One-on-one with Katrina, by phone or video.

  1. 01

    Your skin, explained

    Talk through what is going on with your skin and what has or has not worked for you before, from breakouts and texture to dullness or an event coming up.

  2. 02

    Your current products

    Show what you use now. You will look at your routine together and sort out what to keep, what to change and what to add.

  3. 03

    Your plan

    You will leave with a plan built around your skin and your lifestyle. It may include home care, in-studio treatments or both.

What you leave with

A plan for your skin. Not a guess.

A virtual consultation turns “I don’t know what my skin needs” into a clear next step, whether that is a home routine, a treatment or both.

How to prepare

Have these ready, get more from it.

  1. 1

    Your products

    Have your current skincare nearby, or photos of the labels.

  2. 2

    Good light

    Natural light from a window works best if you choose video.

    A clean face

    Come makeup-free if you choose video, so your skin is easy to see.

  4. 4

    Your questions

    Write down anything you want to ask so nothing is missed.

Questions

Virtual consultations, answered.

Have another question? Email abkskinn@gmail.com.

How does a virtual consultation work?

It is a one-hour, one-on-one appointment by phone or video. You talk through your skin concerns, look at the products you use now, and leave with a personalized plan.

How much does it cost?

A virtual consultation is $100 for one hour. The fee is applied toward any products or treatments you purchase.

Who will I meet with?

Virtual consultations are with Katrina, the founder of ABK SKIN.

Do I need to live in Charlotte?

No. A virtual consultation is a good fit if you cannot make it to the studio.

How do I book?

Open the ABK SKIN booking page and tap Book Appointment. Choose the Virtual tab at the top, then Consultations, then Virtual Skin Consultation. Pick a time, add your details and pay to confirm.

What if I would rather come in person?

Book the in-studio skin consultation instead. It is 60 minutes, one-on-one, at the studio in Charlotte.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancellations made less than 24 hours before an appointment are subject to a $50 charge.
