In the booking menu, choose any category, then Consultation.

What happens in a consultation

In your consultation you will discuss your skin concerns, review the products you are currently using and look closely at your routine.

Together you will build a plan around your skin's needs and goals, which may include in-studio treatments, home care or both.

  • A conversation about your skin goals
  • A review of your current products and routine
  • A personalized plan

Who it’s for

  • First-time clients
  • Anyone unsure which treatment to book
  • Planning a series such as microneedling or peels

How to prepare

Bring your current products or photos of the labels, a short history of past treatments, and any event dates you are planning around. Our guide What to ask at your first skincare consultation has a full checklist.
